# Platform and legal risk checklist

Qualified Indian and launch-market counsel must review DPDP duties and rules, age/child safety, consent/notice, grievance and deletion, intermediary/UGC obligations, law-enforcement handling, cross-border processors, employment/moderator welfare, contest/marketing claims, and incident notification.

Music counsel must review metadata, artwork, lyrics, previews, fingerprinting, caching, sharing and every provider term. Metadata matching is not permission to reproduce audio. Spotify policy forbids content synchronization; YouTube Music support cannot be scraped into existence.

Store readiness requires progressive permission explanations, privacy nutrition/Data Safety declarations, account deletion, report/block/filtering, moderation response, contact details and non-anonymous mutual chat. Do not market background reliability or precise distance. Partner-branded metro/flight/event modes require operator permission and separate safety review.
